**Signature scheme comparison:**

| Scheme | Type | Sig Size | Quantum Safe | NIST Status |
|--------|------|----------|--------------|-------------|
| ECDSA (secp256k1) | Elliptic curve | 64 bytes | ✗ NO | Legacy |
| ML-DSA-65 | Lattice | 2.4 KB | ✓ YES | FIPS 204 |
| SLH-DSA/SHAKE-256s | Hash-based | 22 KB | ✓ YES | FIPS 205 |
| Trifold (this project) | Hash-based | ~25 KB | ✓ YES | Research |

**Why hash-based?** Hash-based signatures are the most conservative post-quantum choice: minimal assumptions (hash collision resistance only), 47 years of cryptanalysis since Merkle's 1979 proposal, and Bitcoin-native (SHA-256 is already trusted and hardware-accelerated).
