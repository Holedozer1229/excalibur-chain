How it works — no DMs, no trust, just code

  1. Run the kit (self-serve/): it builds your BTC HTLC funding transaction. 1 EXCAL lot takes exactly 100,000,000 sats; the 1,000 EXCAL lot takes any amount you choose; the 0.001 EXCAL pilot takes exactly 100,000 sats.
  2. Sign in your own wallet and broadcast. Your funding carries a 1,000-sat signal and an OP_RETURN tag (EXCAL1 or EXCAL1K) with your delivery key.
  3. The watcher verifies your funding and publishes your encrypted claim seed. Decrypt it, take the secret from the BTC claim, and claim your EXCAL on the fork. If anything goes wrong, your BTC refunds to you after the timeout.

Why this is real

  • All three lots are funded on-chain on the Excalibur fork (blocks 42,900, 42,899 and 43,571) — the EXCAL is locked, not promised.
  • QHTLC locks: hash-based, quantum-resistant Trifold signatures, not ECDSA.
  • Opcode-free locks, AuxPoW merge-mined with Bitcoin. The fork split from Bitcoin at block 968,698.
  • Only the first verified funding per lot is served. The secret is the same for everyone per lot, so the first funded HTLC wins and later fundings refund.
